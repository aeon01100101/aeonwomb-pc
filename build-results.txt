Aeon Womb — for PC, v0.5.0 (Oct 22-23 2026)
================================================
THE TOWN UPDATE. Charles City, Iowa moves in around her
house, and so does a 1953 Bel Air with a full working
simulation under it. Mom mode is no longer a room — it's
a life in a town.

NEW IN v0.5.0 — the town of Charles City, Iowa:
  - Her house is a real house now (siding, shingles, porch,
    chimney) with THREE working hinge doors — front, back,
    and a garden gate — that swing open as she approaches.
  - Streets on a loop with two traffic cars, neighbour
    houses, picket fences, waving laundry, swaying trees.
  - Main Street: Betty's Bakery, The Grocer's, the Post
    Office, Doyle's Diner, the Public Library — brick
    storefronts with painted signs and awnings.
  - The Charles Theatre with a chasing-bulb marquee
    ("NOW SHOWING — THE MUSIC MAN"), the Floyd County
    courthouse whose clock keeps real time, a white church,
    Central Park with a gazebo, the Cedar River (animated
    water, one footbridge), a railroad with flashing
    crossing signals, the Co-Op grain elevator and silos,
    two Oliver tractors, and the CHARLES CITY water tower
    with a blinking beacon.
  - Named townsfolk, all animated: Betty waves, Mr. Doyle
    waves, Mr. Patterson walks his mail route, Mrs.
    Kowalski knits on her porch, Little Timmy and Biscuit
    the dog play. Nothing in town sits frozen.

NEW IN v0.5.0 — the 1953 Bel Air, simulated properly:
  - Drive her: E in/out, Enter to crank, G through
    P R N D, W/S/A/D. The Powerglide shifts itself
    (3.10 / 1.95 / 1.30) with real torque cuts.
  - FUEL IS REAL: a 16-gallon tank, burn computed from
    throttle, rpm and mixture — live MPG on the dash,
    trip + odometer persist, hold F at the Main Street
    pumps to refuel.
  - CARBURETOR + CHOKE (MSC rules): the mixture screw
    ([] keys, Park only) leans and riches her — and it
    DRIFTS as she vibrates down the road. Lean + a cold
    engine + no choke = she cranks and dies. The choke
    (C) saves a cold start and floods a warm rich one,
    and left out warm it fouls the plugs.
  - VARIABLE MOUSE THROTTLE (V): pointer-lock mouse is
    the gas pedal — push for more, it stays where you
    leave it, wheel trims it.
  - HARD SHIFTS: a mistuned carb makes the Powerglide
    bang its shifts — longer torque cut, a jolt through
    the camera, counted on the dash.
  - 6-VOLT ELECTRICS: the starter draws 175 A, the
    generator only earns its keep at revs (~10 A at
    3,000 rpm, 6.8 V), headlights cost 9 A with the
    engine off. Run it flat and she clicks at you.
  - WEAR AND TEAR: engine, gearbox, brakes, tires,
    battery health, plugs, oil miles — every system
    ages by how she's treated (cold starts, redline,
    old oil, hard shifts, braking energy). Key I shows
    the condition card in plain language.
  - STATION SERVICES at the pumps, engine off: F fuel,
    O oil change, Y tune-up, J battery charge.
  - EVERY FEATURE ANIMATES: the driver door swings, the
    hood lifts over a real engine bay, the column lever
    swings per gear, signals blink and self-cancel (Z/X),
    the vacuum wipers sweep slower the harder you lean
    on the throttle (U — a real '53 quirk), headlight
    beams fade in (L), the horn is dual-tone (H), the
    body pitches and rolls on its suspension, and the
    tailpipe puffs — white when cold, dark when rich.

NEW IN v0.5.0 — the FusionFall visual pass (home/town):
  - Warm ACES tone mapping outdoors, a gradient sky dome,
    long soft shadows, and painted procedural textures
    for grass, asphalt, concrete, siding, shingles and
    leaves. One colour-pipeline bug found and fixed on
    the way: the new textures must decode as sRGB or the
    renderer brightens them twice and the town washes
    pale. The Bel Air's paint went clearcoat.

VERIFICATION (headless Chromium, log files are the
verdict): the drive-systems probe passes cold-start
deaths (lean, no choke), choke starts, generator amps,
headlight draw, station charging, wear round-trips,
door/hood/lever/signal/wiper animation — and the hard
shift lands (dedicated probe, PASS). The full CDP suite
covers the town, the drive and all four graphics eras.
The brake was accused and acquitted: measured decel
matches the design (4.4 m/s^2) — earlier readings were
the software renderer's slow clock.
  Owned bugs this build: a bulk rename broke the car
  body twice (fixed, screenshot-proven), a services rule
  slammed the hood in Neutral (fixed), and the "hard
  shift failure" chased for an evening turned out to be
  a traffic car pacing her at 12 mph — traffic now
  eases ahead when she comes up behind it.

HONEST LIMITS: headless test rendering runs in slow
motion (software GPU) — her real machine runs full
speed. The car's body is still our procedural '53; the
real '56 Bel Air mod model (downloaded, census taken)
swaps in next, bone-stock as its modder built it.

Aeon Womb — for PC, v0.4.0 (Oct 22 2026)
================================================
Stack: custom JS simulation (faithful port of the Android
GameWorld.java) + Three.js r149 (vendored, MIT) for WebGL rendering
+ WebAudio synthesis + speechSynthesis for mom's voice. Plain
HTML/CSS/JS — no build step, no downloads, offline-capable
(the webcam gesture source uses only the browser's own
getUserMedia; nothing leaves the machine).

NEW IN v0.4.0 — mama mode FPOV + gesture support:
  - FPOV MOM MODE (his ask): Mom mode now opens through her
    own eyes and stays there by default (persisted choice).
    * camera rides her eye point (head-bone space, ~1.617 m);
    * her head cluster AND neck hide in first person (you
      can't see your own neck), everything else of her stays:
      look down and see her dress, apron, bump, brooch, and
      the heart — and the rig's baby-kick pulses read from
      the inside;
    * WASD / arrow keys walk her around the room (1.45 m/s),
      mouse-look via pointer lock on click, drag-look without
      it; she turns to face where she looks; a gentle head
      bob while walking; walls clamp her in, and the sofa and
      TV are circle-collider obstacles;
    * she starts FPOV facing INTO the room (window/TV side),
      not at the wall behind her display pose;
    * 🎥 Third person button (top bar) restores the orbit
      camera and her head; 👀 brings FPOV back.
  - INPUT LAYER (the architectural support he asked for):
    the game no longer reads raw keys in gameplay code — an
    Input object holds intent (moveX / moveZ / turn), polled
    per frame. Keyboard/mouse are source #1. Any future
    source (gamepad, depth camera, a real Kinect via a local
    bridge) writes the same three numbers and just works.
  - GESTURE SOURCE (Kinect-style, built in, source #2):
    🤚 Gestures button (Mom-mode top bar) opens the webcam
    (getUserMedia, 320x240) and runs frame-difference motion
    zones on a 96x54 luminance grid at ~9 Hz:
      motion weighted to a side of frame  -> turn that way
      busy LOW frame (stepping in place)  -> walk forward
      big HIGH frame (both hands up)      -> pat the bump
        (she answers with a mom-voice line, throttled)
    A mirrored picture-in-picture preview sits in the corner
    with a legend. Fully guarded: no mediaDevices = a kind
    "needs localhost" note; permission denied / no camera =
    "denied" state, keyboard unaffected, zero page errors.
    WOMB.gestureSim(zone) injects synthetic zone energy so
    the whole chain (zones -> mapping -> Input -> movement)
    is test-verifiable without a camera.

Carries forward v0.3.x: the KH1-detail room and Aerith mom
(2048² face, 19,904 verts, 3.3-3.7 MB model, exactly-180-bone
animated rig), the womb in four graphics eras, fetus mode,
the VRChat-style Quick Menu, mom's TTS voice.

Known honest limits:
  - Gesture recognition is MOTION-ZONE based (like early
    Kinect minigames), not skeletal joint tracking — it
    reads where movement happens in frame, not which limb
    moved. The Input layer is where a real tracker would
    land later.
  - The webcam needs a secure context: launching via
    start.sh / start.bat (http://localhost) works; opening
    index.html as a file:// may block the camera (the game
    itself still runs, gestures report "unsupported").
  - FPOV walking currently lives in Mom mode; the womb's
    swim still reads its original key path (the Input layer
    is ready for it to adopt next).
  - Verified under SwiftShader software rendering only —
    the real-GPU / real-webcam verdict on Isaiah's PC is
    still his to give.
  - NOT published anywhere — release needs Isaiah's go-ahead.
